## Implications for OpsPilot — inference

### 1. The product cannot win as “better reporting AI”

Power Query, Power BI Copilot and FP&A suites already occupy data transformation, reporting, planning and AI-assistance surfaces. A generic insight-generation pitch will be easily substituted or perceived as redundant.

### 2. The narrow differentiation must be operational proof

The defensible hypothesis is not a new dashboard. It is a controller workflow that verifies data quality, reconciles scenarios, exposes source lineage and moves a cross-functional investigation toward a human-owned action.

### 3. The benchmark question for every interview

> In the last difficult variance investigation, where exactly did the current stack fail: data preparation, reconciliation, evidence/traceability, explanation, ownership, or follow-through?

If the answer is “nowhere material,” OpsPilot should not compete for that account/workflow.

### 4. Implication for roadmap

Do not build direct connectors, generic chat, forecast models or action automation to match broad platforms. First prove the discrete investigation gap with the workflow-displacement protocol.
