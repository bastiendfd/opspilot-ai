# Initial ICP — Validation Hypothesis

**This is not a market fact or final segmentation. It is an interview recruitment hypothesis.**

## Primary ICP hypothesis

A German industrial SME/mid-market manufacturer (roughly 100–1,000 employees) with a recurring monthly Actual/Budget/Forecast process maintained across ERP exports and spreadsheets, where a controller must reconcile and explain material operational-cost deviations to finance and operational stakeholders.

## Required signals to qualify an interview/pilot candidate

1. Recurring reporting/forecast cadence with at least monthly variance investigation.
2. Multiple source files/systems or meaningful manual reconciliation.
3. A real decision consequence when explanations are late or uncertain.
4. A controller/Head of Controlling willing to describe the workflow without sharing confidential data.
5. A plausible route to an explicitly authorised synthetic or sanitised proof of concept later.

## Disqualifying signals for v0

- no recurring variance-investigation workflow;
- data is only high-level and no source traceability is needed;
- mandatory direct SAP integration is required before any test;
- customer cannot consider an external/local prototype because of policy;
- the issue is primarily planning-model redesign rather than reconciliation/investigation.

## Buyer and user hypotheses

| Role | Expected role | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial Controller | Primary user/champion | Frequency, time burden, trust criteria, current workaround |
| Head of Controlling | Sponsor/economic buyer | Capacity, reporting risk, budget process, adoption requirements |
| CFO | Economic buyer/executive sponsor | Measurable outcome, risk, procurement/security bar |
| Procurement / Operations leader | Investigation stakeholder | Whether finance insight reliably translates to an owned action |
| IT / data lead | Gatekeeper/enabler | Integration/security constraints and deployment viability |

## Initial segment order

1. Germany — industrial controlling/operations, file-first workflow.
2. France — same use case, only after German wording/workflow is understood.
3. DACH/Benelux — expand only where language, data-handling and sales evidence support it.

## Key test

Can OpsPilot save meaningful monthly investigation time *without* asking the customer to replace SAP, BI or planning systems—and can it make the output more trusted than the current process?
