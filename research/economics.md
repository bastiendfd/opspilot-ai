# Initial Economics — Hypotheses

**Status:** a planning model, not validated pricing or customer ROI.

## Capacity-savings scenario

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---:|
| Controllers affected | 4 |
| Time saved per controller per month | 15 hours |
| Total time saved per month | 60 hours |
| Total time saved per year | 720 hours |
| Assumed fully loaded labour cost | €75/hour |
| Illustrative annual capacity value | €54,000/year |

The calculation is `4 × 15 × 12 × €75 = €54,000`. The €75/h labour cost is an explicit placeholder; it must be adjusted with customer context and should not be presented as a customer-specific ROI claim.

## Questions before pricing

1. Is time saving the right value metric, or is decision speed/error avoidance more valuable?
2. Does a controller have authority/budget, or does value need to be packaged for CFO/COO/IT?
3. What implementation and security burden reduces willingness to pay?
4. Does a file-first pilot create enough evidence to justify an integration phase?
