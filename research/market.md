# Market Research Log

**Status:** research in progress. This document will distinguish sourced facts from inference and hypotheses.

## Research question

Is there a painful, frequent and monetisable gap between spreadsheet/ERP/BI-based industrial performance reporting and broad AI/data-platform offerings for European manufacturers?

## Initial research lenses

- controller workflow: frequency, duration, error cost, current tools and decision latency;
- buyer economics: capacity, implementation effort, security expectations and budget owner;
- product alternatives: ERP/FP&A/BI/process intelligence/AI copilots;
- differentiation: deterministic reconciliation, source traceability, industrial semantic model and human workflow;
- geography: European hosting, languages, GDPR/AI Act expectations.

## Evidence standard

- **FACT:** directly supported by linked source or recorded interview.
- **INFERENCE:** reasoned interpretation of facts, explicitly labelled.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** proposition to validate; no claim of evidence.
