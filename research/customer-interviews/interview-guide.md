# Customer Discovery Interview Guide

**Purpose:** understand existing behaviour and consequences before pitching a solution.  
**Target:** 15–20 real interviews across industrial controllers, Heads of Controlling/CFOs, operations/plant managers, transformation leaders, SAP consultants and supply-chain managers.

## Before the interview

- obtain permission before outreach or contact;
- state that this is research, not a sales call;
- do not collect confidential employer/customer data;
- ask for consent before recording or attributing quotes;
- research only public company context; do not assume systems, budget or pain.

## Opening (2 minutes)

> I am researching how industrial teams prepare and investigate recurring performance reporting. I am not selling a product today. I am interested in your current process, including what works well. Please avoid confidential figures or documents.

## Core questions

1. Please walk me through your last monthly reporting or performance-review cycle from source data to management discussion.
2. What specifically triggered the work? Who asked for it and who uses the result?
3. Which systems, exports, spreadsheets and people are involved at each handoff?
4. Which step took the longest in the last cycle? Can you describe an example?
5. Where do numbers most often fail to reconcile or require rework?
6. When a material deviation appears, how do you investigate it? What dimensions do you drill into?
7. How do you check that a management comment or conclusion is correct?
8. How often does this happen, and how does that frequency change at month/quarter/year end?
9. Roughly how much time and how many people are involved? What makes that estimate uncertain?
10. What happens when the analysis is late, incomplete or wrong?
11. Which tools, reports or automation have you tried? What worked and what did not?
12. What information would have to be visible for you to trust an automated analysis?
13. Who else is affected and who could approve a change to this process?
14. If this workflow improved materially, what would be the measurable outcome?
15. Is there anything I failed to ask about this process?

## Optional late-stage probes (only after workflow is clear)

- Could you show a *sanitised public/synthetic* example of the shape of data—not the data itself?
- What would make an external tool unacceptable: security, integration, accuracy, auditability, cost, change management, or something else?
- Would you consider testing a local/synthetic demonstration? Why or why not?
- If discussing budget, ask: “How are tools solving this type of problem evaluated and funded?” Do not anchor price first.

## Avoid

- “Would you use an AI that automatically…?”
- “Would €X/month be acceptable?” before a demonstrated cost/value context.
- showing a demo early and then treating positive reactions as validation;
- treating one enthusiastic contact as segment evidence.

## After interview

Write one record from `README.md`’s template. Separate direct quotes, observation, inference and follow-up. Log disconfirming evidence with equal prominence.
