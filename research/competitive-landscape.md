# Competitive Landscape — v0

**Retrieval date:** 2026-08-17  
**Scope:** first-pass, official-page mapping of alternatives relevant to OpsPilot’s performance-intelligence wedge. It is not a funding or pricing database.

## Evidence rules and limits

- **FACT** below is limited to what the retrieved official page explicitly described or titled.
- **INFERENCE** is our strategic interpretation, not a claim by the competitor.
- **UNVERIFIED** means no reliable claim is made yet. Funding, headquarters/country, named customers and pricing are deliberately left blank unless directly sourced in a later research pass.
- Several official sites used anti-bot protection or had inaccessible/changed pages at retrieval; that is a collection limitation, not evidence about their product.

## Initial strategic conclusion

**FACT:** The retrieved landscape contains broad enterprise AI/BI, process intelligence, FP&A and operational-workflow offerings.  
**INFERENCE:** “AI insight generation” alone is not defensible. The testable wedge is a very specific *controller investigation loop*: validate imports → reconcile scenarios → prove the driver with source rows → route a human-reviewed follow-up.  
**HYPOTHESIS:** European industrial mid-market customers may accept this faster than a broad enterprise platform only if it demonstrably reduces monthly manual work and does not create unacceptable security/integration burden.
