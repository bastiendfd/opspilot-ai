# Opportunity Hypotheses

## O-01 — Verified monthly variance investigation

**Hypothesis:** Industrial controllers will value a tool that removes repeated manual reconciliation and gives source-traceable variance attribution across Actual/Budget/Forecast.

- Frequency: typically monthly, sometimes weekly — **to validate**
- Value mechanism: faster, more reliable management commentary/action
- Buyer: Head of Controlling/CFO — **to validate**
- Differentiation candidate: validation + lineage + industrial workflow, not generic generation
- Disproof signal: users view Excel/Power BI as adequate or data extraction as the true unsolved bottleneck

## O-02 — Cross-functional action follow-through

**Hypothesis:** A verified variance becomes more valuable when it can be assigned, reviewed and checked next period.

- Dependency: O-01 must prove useful first
- Risk: becomes a generic task tracker
- Validation question: do users lose value between identifying a variance and closing an action?

## O-03 — European industrial trust layer

**Hypothesis:** local language, deployment/data-location choices and defensible auditability matter more to a European industrial buyer than a generic AI copilot.

- Risk: could be table stakes rather than moat
- Validation question: does it affect selection or merely procurement acceptance?
