# Initial Personas — Hypotheses Pending Interviews

## Industrial Controller (primary user/champion)

- **Context:** monthly closing, actual/budget/forecast reconciliation, management commentary.
- **Pain hypothesis:** loses time collecting, checking and explaining variances across exports.
- **Desired outcome:** reliable explanation with evidence quickly enough to influence the review cycle.
- **Trust requirement:** must inspect source data and correct conclusions.

## Head of Controlling / CFO (economic buyer hypothesis)

- **Context:** accountable for performance visibility, reporting reliability and finance team capacity.
- **Desired outcome:** fewer manual reconciliation cycles and earlier, trusted escalation of material performance risks.
- **Buying concern:** data security, implementation burden, proven ROI and overlap with existing stack.

## Procurement / Operations Manager (investigation stakeholder)

- **Context:** owns a contributor to cost, delivery or volume deviation.
- **Desired outcome:** a specific, defensible question/action rather than an unexplained finance variance.
- **Trust requirement:** the claim must distinguish a data-supported driver from a causal hypothesis.

## Plant / COO / Transformation leader (sponsor hypothesis)

- **Context:** seeks faster operating decisions and follow-through.
- **Desired outcome:** cross-functional action on recurring performance problems.
- **Risk:** may reject another dashboard or generic AI assistant.

All statements above are **assumptions**, not interview findings.
